- **Calendars and contacts on a Nextcloud or CalDAV server.** A new kind of account, under Add Account › Nextcloud or CalDAV: give the server's address, your user name and an app password, and every calendar and address book the server holds comes down and is kept in step, the calendars with their names, colours and rights. Appointments made, moved, changed or deleted here go up to the server; a change made on the server meanwhile wins. Series come with their rule and their occurrences, and a day changed on the server is shown as changed. Contacts are read. Changing one day of a series, answering an invitation and writing contacts are not done for this kind of server yet, and say so.
